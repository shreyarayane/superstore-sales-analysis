# superstore-sales-analysis
A Data Analytics project using Python, SQL concepts, data visualization, and Power BI to analyze sales and business performance.
## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and business trends. Python, SQL concepts, data visualization, and Power BI will be used to analyze the data and create an interactive dashboard.

## Problem Statement

Businesses need to understand which products, categories, and regions generate the highest sales and profit. This project aims to identify sales trends, analyze business performance, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve business decisions.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- SQL
- Power BI
- Git and GitHub

## Dataset Description

The project will use the Sample Superstore sales dataset. It contains information about customer orders, product categories, sales, profit, discounts, and geographical regions.

## Data Cleaning Process

The following data preparation steps will be performed:

1. Inspect the dataset structure and column data types.
2. Identify and handle missing values.
3. Check for duplicate records.
4. Correct data types where necessary.
5. Check for incorrect or inconsistent values.
6. Prepare the cleaned dataset for exploratory data analysis (EDA).

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Exploratory Data Analysis will be performed to understand the dataset and identify important patterns and trends.

The analysis will include:

1. Calculating descriptive statistics such as mean, median, and standard deviation.
2. Analyzing sales and profit across different product categories.
3. Comparing sales performance across regions.
4. Studying the relationship between sales, profit, and discounts.
5. Identifying missing values, duplicate records, and potential outliers.
6. Examining trends to understand business performance.

The findings will be documented after analyzing the actual dataset.

## Data Visualizations

The project will include the following visualizations:

1. Sales by Product Category – to compare category performance.
2. Profit by Region – to identify high-performing regions.
3. Monthly Sales Trend – to understand changes in sales over time.
4. Top 10 Products by Sales – to identify the best-selling products.
5. Sales and Profit Comparison – to understand the relationship between sales and profitability.

Each visualization will include a meaningful title, appropriate labels, and a brief explanation of the findings.

## Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard will be developed to present important sales and business performance metrics.

The dashboard will include:

- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Orders.
- **Sales Analysis:** Sales by category and sub-category.
- **Regional Analysis:** Sales and profit by region.
- **Time Analysis:** Monthly sales trends.
- **Interactive Filters:** Region, category, and order date.

The dashboard will help users explore sales performance and identify important business trends.

## Business Insights

The following business questions will be explored during the analysis:

1. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
2. Which regions perform best in terms of sales and profit?
3. How do sales change over time?
4. Which products contribute most to overall sales?
5. How are discounts related to profitability?

The final insights will be updated after analyzing the actual dataset.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project will aim to recommend:

1. Improving marketing strategies for high-performing product categories.
2. Reviewing discount policies for products with low profitability.
3. Focusing on regions with opportunities for sales growth.
4. Using sales trends to improve inventory and business planning.
